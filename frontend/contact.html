A smoother support process

Three steps to a clearer resolution.

A well-described request helps the team understand the problem and identify the next appropriate step.

STEP 01

Describe the issue

Tell us what you were doing, what happened, and what you expected to happen. Include a relevant reference number if you have one.

STEP 02

Allow a review

The relevant team can assess the details, request additional information if necessary, and explain the available next steps.

STEP 03

Keep the reference

Keep your email and any ticket or transaction reference. Use the same thread when following up on the same issue.
