Membership essentials

Clear rules make a better membership experience. A radical concept, apparently.

01

Wallet-based purchasing

Membership purchases should be paid from your available PTW wallet balance, subject to the backend's eligibility checks.

02

Verified account records

Your displayed membership status should come from your authenticated account, not a value stored only in the browser.

03

Transparent pricing

Membership prices on this page use USD. No exchange-rate conversions are performed by the interface.

This page does not fabricate membership plans or trigger an unverified purchase. Purchase actions should only be enabled after matching the exact membership purchase endpoint and request format implemented by the PTW backend.
